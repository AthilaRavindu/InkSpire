TATTOO STYLES

BLACKWORK

Bold solid black fills, geometric shapes, and graphic patterns. Zero color, maximum impact.

TRADITIONAL

Sailor Jerry roots. Bold outlines, limited palette, timeless subjects — roses, anchors, eagles.

JAPANESE

Irezumi tradition. Dragons, koi, waves, and cherry blossoms in flowing compositions.

GEOMETRIC

Sacred geometry, mandalas, dotwork. Precise linework that creates patterns from math.

WATERCOLOR

Painterly, borderless designs that mimic watercolor technique. Vibrant, dreamy, unique.

NEO-TRADITIONAL

Classic subjects, modern depth. Richer shading, wider color range, illustrative detail.
