produces these automatically._

**How to use each pin:** generate the image from IMAGE PROMPT, then paste the title,
description, board and URL into pinterest.com/content/scheduled. Post the two pins of a
day **a few hours apart**, not back to back.

**Paste the whole URL including the `?utm_...` part.** That is what attributes the
traffic to Pinterest instead of burying it under "direct".

**Add `--ar 2:3` at the end if you use Midjourney.** Other tools: set the aspect ratio
or canvas to 1000 × 1500 before generating.
